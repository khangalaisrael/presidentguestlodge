Welcome

A quiet stay in the middle of town

President Guest Lodge sits on President Street in Louis Trichardt, under the Soutpansberg mountains. Behind the gate you'll find a thatched lapa, a pool and rooms with their own en-suite bathrooms.

Stopping over on the N1, in town for work, or here to explore Hangklip Forest and Ben Lavin Nature Reserve: you're close to all of it.
